Make slides in Canva

Two ways to do it — both need a Canva account (the free one or your school's paid plan), on a computer.

Best match to your lesson

Downloads your finished slides and opens Canva. In Canva, drag the file onto the page (or click Upload) — it becomes editable slides with your exact lesson content, ready to restyle.

Let Canva's AI design it from a prompt

Copies a ready-made prompt about your topic and opens Canva's AI presentation maker. Click the prompt box, paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V), and press Enter. Quicker, but the wording comes out more generic than your lesson.

About Lesson Planner

Lesson Planner helps teachers create complete, teachable Value-Education and Bible lessons. Enter a topic, a passage, or both; the AI service you choose (using your own key) writes the lesson, and you can refine it in plain language. Export to Word, PDF, or PowerPoint, build a CBSE report for admins, or turn it into Canva slides.

Your settings, unfinished setup, drafts and templates are saved on this device. Lessons are generated by your chosen AI provider; scripture is shown from public-domain translations (or a faithful Hindi rendering) so it prints correctly.
